What we do

Raising the standard of care at the bedside

We want critical care nursing in Sri Lanka to match the best in the world. Four areas of work get us there.

01

Education & CPD

Accredited workshops, seminars and certification that keep ICU nurses current.

02

Research & evidence

Supporting research and innovation, and turning evidence into everyday ICU practice.

03

Standards & advocacy

Speaking for critical care nurses and shaping quality standards in nursing education and care.

04

Community health

Taking health promotion to schools, the airwaves and the streets, in Sinhala and English.
