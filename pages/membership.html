Benefits

What members receive

All categories share the same benefits.

  • Free or discounted registration for workshops
  • Access to exclusive workshops
  • Letters confirming your membership for travel and other purposes, and certificates of your skills
  • Opportunities to take part in international organisations and conferences
  • A membership ID card (lifetime card for Lifetime members)
  • Referrals to courses at local and foreign universities and academic institutions, sometimes at a discount

How to join

Four simple steps

  1. Choose a category

    Use the cards above, or the helper if you're unsure.

  2. Pay the fee

    Bank transfer or deposit, then keep the slip or a screenshot.

  3. Apply online

    Fill in the form, upload your photo and payment slip, and get a reference number.

  4. Get your number

    The committee reviews your application and emails your membership number once approved.

Payment details

Pay the fee for your category to this account, and keep the slip or a screenshot to upload with your application.
