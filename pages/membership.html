How to join

Four simple steps

  1. Apply online

    Fill in the form below with your professional details.

  2. Upload documents

    Add a photo and a copy of your nursing registration.

  3. Pay the fee

    Pay by bank transfer and upload the receipt.

  4. Welcome aboard

    The committee reviews your application and confirms by email.

Application

Membership application

It takes about five minutes. Fields marked optional can be left blank. Your details are used only to process your membership.
