Taking care beyond the ICU

Many ICU admissions could have been prevented earlier. So we take health knowledge to schools, the radio and the streets.

School Health Programme

අකුරට සවිය: first-minute skills for students

Our nurses visit schools to teach emergency response and first aid, and to talk about healthy habits. Students practise hands-on skills and receive certificates for taking part.

  • Emergency response and basic first aid
  • Recognising when someone needs urgent help
  • Healthy lifestyle and prevention
