Who we are

Nurses who care for the most critically ill

The Sri Lanka Intensive Care Nurses' Association (SLICNA) is a professional organisation dedicated to advancing critical care nursing in Sri Lanka.

SLICNA was founded in 2022 to raise standards in Sri Lanka's intensive care units. We aim to set quality standards in nursing education, help improve healthcare in Sri Lanka, and bring critical care nursing up to global standards.

We run workshops, seminars and research programmes that give nurses the knowledge and skills they need. With membership growing across the country, we play a key role in improving patient care and promoting evidence-based practice in intensive care.

Our motto, "Unity for Humanity", is drawn in our emblem: two open hands around a heart and its heartbeat, the care that critical care nurses give every shift.

Our vision

To be recognised as a global leader in critical care nursing, elevating standards of care.

Our mission

  • Promote excellence through education, training and certification
  • Support research and innovation in ICU nursing
  • Advocate evidence-based practices to improve patient outcomes
